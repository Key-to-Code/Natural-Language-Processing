The Target Audience & Problem Definition

Whose Problem Is It?

  • Salaried Employees in India: Everyday taxpayers navigating complex Form 16s, Investment Declarations, and dynamic monthly payslips.
  • Gig Workers & Freelancers: Individuals filing without a centralized corporate HR team to assist them.
  • First-Time Taxpayers: Fresh graduates entering the workforce who lack fundamental financial literacy.

Why Are We Solving It?

  • Opaque Frameworks: Documents are designed explicitly for professional Chartered Accountants (CAs) and the Income Tax Department, not regular citizens.
  • Financial Penalties: Everyday citizens routinely overpay taxes or lose rightful savings because they cannot decipher complex sections like 80C, 80D, or House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemptions.
  • Asymmetrical Access: Private, specialized asset management advisory services are heavily gated behind high subscription costs, leaving low-to-middle income groups behind.

Fiduciary Lens: Localization for Indian Tax Landscapes

The System Engine

Accepts PDFs of Indian Form 16 documents, multi-page salary structures, and investment receipts, translating them into crisp, plain-English educational roadmaps.

Real-World Regulatory Grounding

  • Data Ingestion Sources: Parses PDF tax documents using PyMuPDF to maintain block structure and extract reliable text.
  • Semantic Chunking: Employs context-aware chunking to preserve paragraphs and legal clauses using recursive character splitting.
  • Metadata Extraction: Specially isolates regional financial entities: Assessment Years, Acts, and Section references (e.g., 80C) via regex to enrich vector databases.

System Engineering Architecture & Competitors

The NLP Pipeline

PDF Parsing → Recursive Text Chunking → Regex Metadata Extraction → Grounded Indian Tax RAG

Commercial AI Tax Alternatives

Existing Indian platforms like ClearTax, TaxBuddy, and PlanMyTax.ai use AI primarily for automation, extracting Form 16 data to autofill fields and speed up the ITR filing process.

Our Unique Educational Edge

Unlike commercial tools focused purely on extraction, Fiduciary Lens focuses on translation (SDG 4). It breaks down opaque legal jargon (e.g., Section 80C) into plain-English to actively educate the everyday taxpayer.
